KNOW WHO WE ARE

Cradle Saints Nursery and Primary School

Cradle Saints Nursery and Primary School was established on 17th September 2017 by Chief Patrick Onyeachonam Nwokolo and Mrs. Chinenye Phina Nwokolo, his wife. Together, they established the school with a shared vision of providing quality education and nurturing children to become thoughtful, responsible, and future leaders who will positively impact their society and the world.

Mrs. Chinenye Phina Nwokolo is an experienced educator and school administrator. Her desire is to groom pupils through quality education while instilling important values such as compassion, service, and leadership in every pupil.

Mrs. Nwokolo has a strong record of running excellent Nursery and Primary Schools and demonstrates outstanding leadership by example. Her experience and expertise continue to guide the school as we work to nurture pupils and teachers alike, helping them develop the exemplary qualities that have been the hallmark of her career.

She is supported by a team of seasoned teachers who share the same passion, values, and vision for building a lasting legacy of excellence. Together, they are firmly committed to supporting the nurturing, development, and education of ever-evolving generations of pupils.

OUR COMMITMENT

Quality Education, Character and Care

At Cradle Saints Nursery and Primary School, we are committed to providing quality education in a welcoming and enabling environment where every child is given the care and attention needed to grow.

We believe that education goes beyond academic learning. Our children are cared for physically, emotionally and socially, while being guided by values that encourage the fear and love of God, truth, respectfulness and humility.

Through appropriate resources and a supportive learning environment, we strive to add value to each child's life and prepare them for a future of success and distinction.
